## Part A — SEO Hardening (the lead-generation defense)

Driven by `claude-seo` v2.2.4 rules. Your titles and base JSON-LD are already good; the gaps are elsewhere.

### A1. Make city pages pass the "Swap Test" (CRITICAL — doorway-page risk)
The #1 risk the SEO skill flags: city pages that are identical except the city name. One HVAC firm lost 80% of rankings + 63% of traffic to this in the March 2024 Core Update.
- Audit London/Manchester/Birmingham/Bristol/Leeds/Glasgow for swappable content.
- Inject **genuinely local, non-swappable** elements per city: specific routes (M4 corridor, M62), local airports (Heathrow, Manchester, Birmingham, Leeds Bradford, Glasgow), landmarks/jurisdictions (Royal Courts of Justice for London legal, Filton aerospace for Bristol AOG), neighbourhood-level coverage notes.
- Target 60%+ unique content per page (the skill's floor for primary location pages).
- This protects the pages that drive your highest-intent local leads.

### A2. Service pages → dedicated, 800+ words with `Service` schema
The skill is explicit: *"dedicated service pages = #1 local organic factor AND #2 AI visibility factor"* (Whitespark 2026).
- Verify same-day / AOG / medical / legal pages hit 800+ words and 100% unique.
- Add **`Service` JSON-LD** with `serviceType`, `provider` (linked to Org `@id`), `areaServed` (6 cities), `url`. Plus `BreadcrumbList` (Home > Services > X).
- Medical & legal are YMYL-adjacent → add E-E-A-T signals (process disclosure, chain-of-custody language, compliance mentions). No fake credentials.

### A3. Strengthen Organization + add `Service`/`Offer` schema on homepage
- Homepage already has Organization + WebSite + FAQPage JSON-LD. Keep Organization, add `sameAs` (socials/Trustpilot/Companies House when you have them), confirm `contactPoint` + `logo`.
- Hub-and-spoke: homepage links to all 6 cities + 4 services (verify within 3 clicks, no orphans).

### A4. Sitemap + robots cleanup
- Remove `<changefreq>` and `<priority>` from `sitemap.xml` (Google ignores them; signals outdated SEO practice). Keep accurate `<lastmod>`.
- Add AI crawler allowances to `robots.txt`: `GPTBot`, `OAI-SearchBot`, `ClaudeBot`, `PerplexityBot` (brand mentions correlate 3x more with AI visibility than backlinks per Ahrefs).

### A5. Heading hierarchy + internal links audit
- One H1 per page (verify), no skipped levels (H1→H2→H3).
- Question-based H2/H3 headings where natural (AI citation + PAA patterns).
- 3–5 internal links on service pages, links to all children on location pages, descriptive varied anchor text (never "click here").

## Part B — Accessibility & Consistency (scalability foundation)

### B1. `prefers-reduced-motion` site-wide
Add the media query to every page. Disables ticker, pulse, shimmer, and all new Part C animations. Your Next.js version has this; static doesn't. Required for WCAG + protects INP.

### B2. Focus-visible states
`:focus-visible` brass outlines (2px) on all buttons, links, inputs, mobile toggle, across all 22 pages. Keyboard users currently get nothing. Also feeds Google's 2026 "Agentic Browsing" signal.

### B3. Container width standardization
Homepage = 1280px, all other 21 pages = 1200px → visible width shift on navigation. Standardize to 1280px.

### B4. Image/alt + semantic HTML pass
- Decorative SVGs → `aria-hidden="true"`. The one real image (`assets/og-image.jpg`) → confirm dimensions set + alt.
- Verify no `<div onclick>` where a `<button>`/`<a>` belongs.

## Part C — Creative & Animation (the "wow" vs competitors)

All gated behind `prefers-reduced-motion`. Motion follows the UX-repo's tier system: 150ms micro / 300ms standard / 500ms emphasis, named easing curves reused everywhere.

### C1. Live Dispatch hero element (THE differentiator)
Stylized inline-SVG UK map in/under the hero: forest bg, brass hub dots on your 6 cities, animated pulse-rings + faint route arcs drawing between hubs. Driven by your existing `DISPATCH_TICKER_JOBS` array (loop it, plot dots) — no API, never "broken." DPD does live tracking after booking; nobody does it in the marketing hero.

### C2. City-specific hero motifs (SEO pages)
Per-city CSS/SVG accent in the hero band: London skyline + Thames line, Manchester Ship Canal/Metrolink, etc. No stock photos. Makes each page *feel* local → supports the Swap Test fix commercially, not just textually.

### C3. Count-up stat animations
Hero stats (`60m / £20k / 24/7 / 100%`) animate 0→value on scroll into view via `IntersectionObserver`. Cheap, high-impact.

### C4. Magnetic + glow button hovers
Extend `.shimmer-btn`: tiny JS sets `--mouse-x/--mouse-y` → radial brass glow follows cursor; subtle magnetic pull on primary CTAs. CSS-only fallback for reduced motion.

### C5. Staggered card entrances
Fleet / services / sectors / reviews cards fade-up with 60ms stagger on viewport entry (`IntersectionObserver` + CSS).

### C6. Scroll-drawn "How it works" timeline
1→2→3→4 timeline (Book → Dispatch → Track → Delivered); connecting line draws itself, numbers pop in sequence on scroll. Reinforces the 60-min promise.

### C7. Fleet card vehicle silhouettes
Minimalist inline-SVG silhouettes (motorcycle, small van, LWB, Luton) that fill brass on hover. Expands your existing inline-SVG icon approach.

### C8. Carbon estimate in Quote Wizard
Next to revealed quote price, show estimated kg CO₂ (`miles × fleet emission factor`). Small leaf icon, muted styling. DPD says "green" abstractly; you show the real number. **2026 differentiator.**
