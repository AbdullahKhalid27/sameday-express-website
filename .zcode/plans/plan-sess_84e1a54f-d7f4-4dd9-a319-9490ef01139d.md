## PART G — Navbar redesign (premium minimal, site-wide)

**Diagnosis:** Current navbar has 10 links + a loud green phone pill + filled Book button + hamburger, all crammed into 1232px with 1.5rem gaps → the "every button pushed together" feeling. Two different CSS implementations exist (index vs inner pages).

**New design (consistent across all 18 pages):**
- **7 links** (remove Trust; merge Services+Sectors → "Services"): Home · Our Fleet · Services · Trade Accounts · About · FAQ · Contact
- **Logo:** refined — 38px forest mark with brass border, slightly tighter type, brass-dark subtitle ("Couriers")
- **Links:** uppercase 0.82rem, 0.07em tracking, `gap: 2rem`, brass underline animates width on hover (cubic-bezier), muted→forest color shift. Centered with `margin: auto`.
- **Phone:** demoted from loud green pill → quiet icon-link (phone SVG + green number, pulses subtly). Icon-only on mobile (<768px).
- **CTA:** ONE primary "Book Online" button (forest bg, brass border, 6px radius) with lift+shadow on hover. The sole filled element.
- **Hamburger:** cleaner, shows <1024px. Mobile nav = full-screen slide-in (left-aligned, premium feel), with the phone + Book button as a CTA stack at the bottom.
- Breakpoint unified at 1024px across all pages (currently index=1100px, inner=768px — inconsistent).
- Apply the SAME CSS to index.html and all 17 inner pages (currently they diverge).

**Result:** `[Logo]  [7 centered links]  [📞 phone-link]  [Book Online]` — one clear focal point, generous breathing room.

## PART M — SEO content (geo-content-optimizer tactics)

Apply to **service pages + city pages** — the AEO play (makes AI engines cite you):

1. **25–50 word answer-block intros** at the top of each page answering the core question directly:
   - AOG page: "What is an AOG courier? A same-day courier service specialized in..."
   - City pages: "A same-day courier in [City] collects within 60 minutes..."
   - Medical/Legal/Same-Day: same pattern.
2. **Specific numbers everywhere** (GEO engines cite specifics, not adjectives): "60-minute collection", "£20,000 goods-in-transit", "24/7/365", fleet capacities.
3. **Question-based H2/H3 headings** where natural (feeds PAA / AI citation).
4. **Title tags** confirmed at 50–60 chars keyword-first (already mostly done).
5. **Readability pass:** shorter sentences in long paragraphs, tighten dense intros, break up walls of text. Improve line-length and scanability.
